# Code of Conduct

CodeGent exists so that more people can learn and use agentic coding, whatever their budget,
background or experience. This project is for everyone who takes part in it in good faith.

## Our standard

Expected:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to programming.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people have different tools, budgets, languages and time zones.
- Say what you tried and what happened when you ask for help, and thank people who help.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or attention, or publishing someone's private information.
- Pressuring people for stars, votes or reciprocal engagement, or spamming the project's spaces
  with promotion.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to people
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile of the repository owner, or
use the repository's private _Report a vulnerability_ form if it is sensitive. Reports are treated
confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in an incident do not decide on it.

This text is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
